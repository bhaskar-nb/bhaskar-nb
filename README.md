# Bhaskar Nakka

I build data analytics solutions that help business teams and decision-makers understand what is happening in their data and make better-informed decisions.

My work focuses on turning raw, sometimes messy datasets into structured analysis, clear business findings, and practical dashboards. I work across the analytics process—from cleaning and querying data to exploring trends, defining KPIs, and presenting results in a way people can use.

## What I Build

- **Business and performance analysis** — investigate revenue, profitability, operations, customers, and trends to answer practical business questions.
- **KPI dashboards and reports** — turn important measures into clear, interactive views that help teams monitor performance.
- **Data preparation and exploration** — clean, validate, and explore datasets before drawing conclusions.
- **SQL analytics and data models** — query and organize data to support reporting and deeper analysis.
- **End-to-end analytics projects** — combine Python, SQL, spreadsheets, and BI tools to move from raw data to useful insights.

I build these projects for **business teams, analysts, and decision-makers** who need clear answers from data—not just charts or numbers without context.

## Main Tech Stack

- **SQL** — joins, aggregations, CTEs, subqueries, window functions, and analytical queries
- **Python** — Pandas, NumPy, data cleaning, and exploratory data analysis
- **Excel** — PivotTables, data analysis, reporting, and dashboards
- **Power BI** — data modeling, KPI reporting, DAX, and interactive dashboards
- **Tableau** — visual analytics and interactive dashboards
- **Git & GitHub** — version control, collaboration basics, and project documentation
